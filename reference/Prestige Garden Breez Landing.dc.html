Overview

Prestige Garden Breez, the Final Chapter (Phase 7) of The Prestige City, sits on Sarjapur–Marathahalli Road (SH-35) at Yamare Village. Spread over 10 acres, it holds 655 units across 4 towers — Tower 1 at Ground+27 floors, Towers 2–4 at Ground+30 — in premium 2 and 3 bed configurations, plus 3 penthouses. Homes are designed with no common walls and three-side open layouts, around 85% landscaped open space.

Part of the wider Prestige City master plan alongside Fernvale, Avalon Park, Aspen Greens, Aston Park, Eden Park, Eaton Park, and Meridian Park.
